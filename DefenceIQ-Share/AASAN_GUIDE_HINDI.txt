======================================================================
               DEFENCEIQ - USER SETUP & SHARING GUIDE (HINDI)
======================================================================

Aapke teeno naye niyam (rules) ko system me implement kar diya gaya hai:
1. "Ek baar laptop connect ho jaaye toh hum kahi bhi hon, phone se connected rahe."
2. "Pairing token tab generate ho jab hum naye laptop ko phone se connect karne ka try kar rahe hon."
3. "Ek baar connect ho gaya toh tab tak connected rahe jab tak hum khud usse disconnect na karein."

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PERMANENT STICKY PAIRING (जब तक आप खुद Unpair न करें)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Phone me app open karte hi ab DIRECT DASHBOARD khulega!
- Chahe:
  * Phone restart ho jaye
  * App close karke dobara open karein
  * Laptop band ho jaye ya network badle
- App aapko kabhi bhi Pairing screen par wapas nahi bhejegi!
- Dashboard par hi status dikhega:
  * 🟢 Connected (Active Now)
  * 🟡 Reconnecting... (Agar network change ho raha ho)
  * 🔴 Laptop Offline (Last seen time ke sath)
- Pairing screen tabhi aayegi jab aap Dashboard par diye gaye
  "Revoke / Unpair" button par khud tap karenge ya naya laptop jodna chahenge.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. KAHI BHI HON, CONNECTION LIVE RAHEGA (GLOBAL CLOUD RELAY)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ab connection sirf Wi-Fi tak seemit nahi hai:
  * Agar aap ghar/office se bahar chale jayein aur phone me Mobile Data (4G/5G) chalu ho,
  * Ya laptop ghar par ho aur aap kisi aur shehar me hon,
- DefenceIQ ka End-to-End Encrypted Cloud Relay background me apne aap
  laptop aur phone ko jod kar rakhega!
- Aap kahi se bhi:
  * Laptop ke open apps live dekh sakte hain
  * Kaun se USB ya devices jude hain dekh sakte hain
  * Time & Date wise alerts aur timeline dekh sakte hain

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. NAYE LAPTOP KE LIYE ON-THE-SPOT TOKEN GENERATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Jab bhi kisi NAYE LAPTOP ko phone se connect karna ho:
  1. Us naye laptop par `Show-Pairing-Token.bat` run karein.
  2. Wahan screen par 'N' press karein -> On the spot ek naya unique
     token (jaise DIQ-XXXX-XXXX) generate ho jayega!
  3. Phone ke Pairing screen me jakar yeh naya token daalein ya
     "Nearby Laptops on Wi-Fi" me 1-tap karke connect karein!
  4. Ek baar connect hone ke baad yeh naya laptop bhi hamesha ke liye
     phone se jud jayega!
